# C.R.E.D.I.T Project Foundry — Day-30 pilot protocol

**Release:** `0.3.1`

**Purpose:** Decide whether the brief-to-dossier proof loop is useful and self-serve enough to justify the 31–60 day migration build.

## Cohort

Recruit five to eight outside founder-led ventures that have a complex platform idea, existing source material and a near-term capital or build decision. Do not count W8R team members toward the five-founder exit threshold.

The operator creates one bounded invitation per participant or a small controlled cohort. Site access remains separately controlled by the private Sites allowlist; an application invitation code does not grant Site access by itself.

## Consent and data boundary

Before a session starts, the participant must acknowledge:

1. private measurement of completion events, duration, assistance, ratings and deliberate feedback;
2. that the product is a prototype and does not move funds, verify identity or activate regulated services;
3. that no private keys, payment data, identity documents, customer lists or commercially sensitive records will be submitted.

The session stores the authenticated Site user ID only for ownership enforcement. The operator API and exported evidence use a pseudonymous participant alias and omit participant emails and account identifiers. A quotation is included only when the participant separately consents to that exact text.

## Standard journey

Every participant completes the same sequence:

1. frame one genuine but sanitised venture brief;
2. compile and review the decision dossier;
3. inspect evidence labels and ownership;
4. read the five investor challenge passes;
5. inspect the MVP, `NOT NOW` boundary and proof runway;
6. change one material input and save another version;
7. download both Markdown and JSON exports;
8. name the decision improved, minutes saved and risk exposed.

Assistance requests and issues are recorded. A participant is “unassisted” only when their completed session has no assistance event.

## Day-30 gates

Advance only when all are true:

- five outside founders complete;
- five name a decision improved, time saved and a risk exposed;
- at least three complete without live assistance;
- median completed-journey duration is ten minutes or less;
- five download both portable exports.

Usefulness and clarity ratings, completion rate, accessibility notes and the issue ledger inform iteration but do not replace these gates.

## Kill or pivot rule

After two focused product iterations, pause expansion when fewer than three of five matched founders complete without live assistance, or when participants cannot restate the wedge and next proof after five minutes. Do not improve the dashboard by weakening a gate or relabelling incomplete activity as traction.

## Operator procedure

1. Confirm the participant has private Site access.
2. Create a single-purpose invitation in `/credit/ops`.
3. Copy the one-time pilot URL and deliver it privately.
4. Do not request confidential or regulated data.
5. Review blocking issues between sessions.
6. Close unused invitations when recruitment ends.
7. Export the investor evidence memo and JSON after the cohort closes.
8. Record the decision to advance, iterate, pivot or stop.

## Product boundary

This protocol evaluates Project Foundry only. W8R commerce, BTLR migration, OfPay quotes and settlement, receipt credentials, identity verification, custody, escrow, insurance and financial reporting remain synthetic, prototype or dependency-gated unless separate evidence changes their status.
