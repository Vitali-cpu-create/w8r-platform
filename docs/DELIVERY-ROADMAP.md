# Evidence-led delivery roadmap

## Project Foundry proof loop (release 0.3.1)

- Structured founder brief and Phase Ω constitutional gate
- Provider-neutral deterministic C.R.E.D.I.T compilation
- Evidence labels, capability graph, adapter contracts and five investor passes
- Versioned private D1 persistence with audit events
- Portable JSON and Markdown dossiers
- Explicit vision, wedge, MVP, expansion and `NOT NOW` boundaries
- Invitation-only founder pilot with explicit consent and safe-data acknowledgements
- Pseudonymous telemetry, feedback, issue ledger and operator evidence room
- Day-30 gate calculation plus portable investor evidence memo

Exit evidence: five outside founders complete a dossier without an AI key and can identify the decision improved, time saved and risk exposed. At least three complete without live assistance, the median completion time is ten minutes or less, and five complete both portable exports. Full acceptance and kill criteria are recorded in `docs/CREDIT-PROJECT-031.md`.

## Phase 0 — Investor and design-partner proof (current)

- Interactive end-to-end product journey
- Synthetic quote, payment, receipt, resale, dispute and reconciliation state
- D1-backed investor workspace and audit trail
- Architecture, security boundaries and regulatory assumptions documented

Exit evidence: investors and merchant operators can complete the story without narration and identify which steps are simulated.

## Phase 1 — Merchant migration MVP

- Recruit 10–20 Australian design partners across physical, digital and collectible goods
- Build authorised Shopify CSV/API importer and dependency map
- Validate catalogue, media, customer-consent and historical-order reconciliation
- Launch seven accessible storefront templates and guided BTLR setup

Exit evidence: at least 90% of supported store records reconcile, with merchant-approved exceptions and a measured time to first publish.

## Phase 2 — Regulated payment sandbox

- Obtain Australian legal and AUSTRAC perimeter advice
- Contract at least two quote/liquidity providers and one fiat settlement partner
- Implement signed quote adapters, circuit breakers, idempotency and ledger accounting
- Complete PCI scope assessment for card paths and independent security testing

Exit evidence: successful sandbox-to-small-production payments, defined loss limits, audited reconciliation and provider failover tests.

## Phase 3 — Receipt vault and protection

- Issue privacy-preserving signed receipt credentials for card, bank and digital-asset purchases
- Implement recovery, rotation, revocation and selective disclosure
- Launch case management, refund ledger and category-specific buyer/seller policy
- Integrate carrier events and serial/condition evidence

Exit evidence: deterministic recovery, no duplicated ownership, tested dispute SLAs and legally reviewed policy.

## Phase 4 — High-value resale

- Contract licensed escrow/custody, authentication and insurance partners
- Pilot receipt-backed fixed-price resale before timed auctions
- Add inspection windows, tracked handover and conditional settlement
- Measure fraud, claims, fulfilment time and resale liquidity

Exit evidence: completed protected resale cycles with matched funds, item, receipt and owner.

## Phase 5 — Platform scale

- Open versioned provider and merchant app SDKs
- Prioritise integrations from measured design-partner dependency data
- Add jurisdiction gates, observability, disaster recovery and support operations
- Expand beyond Australia only after settlement, tax and consumer-law readiness

Exit evidence: repeatable merchant acquisition, positive contribution margin, controlled loss rate and reliable provider redundancy.

## Metrics investors should receive monthly

- Signed merchant design partners, activated merchants and time to first sale
- Imported-record reconciliation rate and manual exception hours
- Checkout conversion by rail, quote expiry and failover rate
- Gross payment volume, take rate, provider cost and contribution margin
- Fraud, chargeback, refund, dispute and insurance loss rates
- Receipt issue, recovery, transfer and resale rates
- Delivery success, support contacts and trust-tier fee discounts
- Uptime, incident severity, recovery time and regulatory milestones
