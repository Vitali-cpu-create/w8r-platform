# OfPay glyph study - round 01

The comparison board is designed for mixing structural elements rather than choosing a finished logo immediately.

1. **Independent** - clearest literal reading of an `f` inside an `O`; safest baseline.
2. **Hook touch** - nearly independent, but the upper hook visually touches the ring.
3. **Right bridge** - the crossbar joins the ring on the right; strong forward movement.
4. **Full bridge** - the crossbar spans the entire O; maximum small-size clarity but more emblematic.
5. **Hook flow** - the f's upper hook becomes the start of an anti-clockwise open ring.
6. **Humanist** - angled stem and rising crossbar add warmth and handwriting character.
7. **Micro bold** - compact proportions intended for checkout buttons, favicons and keyboards.
8. **Soft square** - a contemporary fintech container rather than a literal circular O.
9. **One stroke** - a continuous ribbon idea with the strongest proprietary character.
10. **Open optical** - open O and independent f; avoids both the Q and @ resemblances.

For the next refinement, specify preferred components in a form such as: `7's weight + 6's f + 10's opening`.
